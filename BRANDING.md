# Astute Nexa brand guide

**Nexa** is where your team's work connects. The name comes from *nexus*: one place where documents, numbers, slides, conversations, mail, time and files meet.

**Tagline:** One free workspace your team owns.

## Voice

- Plain and direct. Say what a thing does, in everyday words.
- Confident, never loud. No hype, no exclamation marks in the product.
- Built for people who want to own their tools: honest about limits, proud of control.

## The family

Every app has a one-word name, a colour, and a job.

| App | Job | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| **Write** | Documents | Coral `#FF6B4A` |
| **Grid** | Spreadsheets | Emerald `#10B981` |
| **Deck** | Presentations | Amber `#F59E0B` |
| **Huddle** | Chat and calls | Iris `#7C5CFF` |
| **Relay** | Mail | Sky `#3B82F6` |
| **Cadence** | Calendar | Rose `#EC4899` |
| **Vault** | Files and sync | Teal `#14B8D4` |

Write the app names as one word with a capital letter. In a sentence: "Open it in Grid," "Share it from Vault."

## Core colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Nexa Violet | `#6D4BFF` | Primary buttons, links, focus |
| Signal Cyan | `#22C7E8` | Second accent, highlights |
| Ink | `#141A33` | Text on light |
| Night | `#0A0E1C` | Dark mode background |
| Mist | `#F6F7FB` | Light mode background |

**The Nexa gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #6D4BFF 0%, #3D7BFF 50%, #22C7E8 100%)`. Use it for the logo, the app tile on Home and one hero moment per screen at most.

Status colours: success `#0FA968`, warning `#E08A00`, error `#E5484D`.

## Logo

The mark is a rounded square in the Nexa gradient with a white N drawn as one continuous line, ending in a dot at the top right: a path that connects and arrives. Files: `public/icons/icon.svg`, plus PNGs at 192 and 512 pixels and a maskable version for Android.

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width.
- Don't recolour, rotate, outline or add effects to it.
- Next to the wordmark "Nexa", the mark sits at the height of the capital N plus a little.

## Type

- **Inter** for the interface and documents, falling back to the system font.
- **JetBrains Mono** for formulas and code.
- Sentence case for buttons, menus and headings: "New document", not "New Document".

## Deck themes

Five original slide themes ship with Deck: **Aurora** (dark violet), **Daybreak** (clean white), **Ember** (warm dark), **Glacier** (cool light, serif) and **Terminal** (green on black).

## What not to do

- Don't use Microsoft product names, icons, colours or layouts to describe Nexa apps. Say "documents," not "Word files," except when talking about the `.docx` format itself.
- Don't call Nexa a clone or a copy. It's its own suite that opens and saves the common Office formats.

Before using these names commercially, have them checked for trademark conflicts in your markets.
